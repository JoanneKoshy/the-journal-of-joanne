dear diary, entry no. 2026

dear reader,

hi, I'm Joanne! I'm an AI engineer who builds Generative AI and Agentic AI systems: LLM apps, RAG pipelines, multi-agent workflows and MCP integrations, all the way from a tiny idea to a deployed product.

and yes, I do the DevOps too! I don't just build it and wave goodbye. I ship it to Azure myself, wire up the CI/CD, and stare at the pipeline until it turns green 🟢 (honestly, the best feeling in the world). from my laptop to the cloud, I take my little apps all the way home. ☁️

I'm a builder at heart (software and hardware) and a yapper by nature. I'm always experimenting, always multitasking, and always carrying way too many ideas at once (somehow, most of them get shipped). When I'm not building, I'm usually on a stage explaining AI agents to a room full of people, because I love making tech feel less scary and a lot more fun.

Right now I'm a Decision Scientist at Bayes Compass while finishing my B.Tech in Computer Science at MITS, Kochi. This journal is the story so far. Grab a cold coffee and turn the page. ☕

xo, JoanneAI developer · hardware builder · dreamer

chapter 03

things I've built

systems that reason, retrieve & respect privacy

no. 01 · featured

Federated Banking Intelligence

an AI agent that thinks across 6 banking systems

An enterprise-style agentic AI platform on Azure OpenAI. One LLM agent reasons across six MCP servers (CRM, Core Banking, Risk, Policy, Accounts Payable and AP Policy), picks the right tools, and delegates multi-step workflows, with guardrails that block unauthorized writes and a React UI showing live agent traces.

Azure OpenAIMCPPostgreSQL/NeonSAP BTPReact
no. 02

AI Compliance Advisor

reads the rules so you don't have to

Hybrid RAG that gives grounded compliance analysis across GDPR, the EU AI Act, India DPDP and NIST AI RMF. It combines dense and sparse retrieval with a rule-based jurisdiction engine, keeps every claim tied to a real source, and ships an executive dashboard with PDF reports and audit trails.

FastAPIAzure OpenAIChromaDBBM25React
no. 03

Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning

medical AI that never sees your data

A federated deep-learning pipeline built with Flower that trains a CNN pneumonia detector across simulated hospitals without sharing patient data, benchmarking centralized vs. FedAvg training on non-IID data.

FlowerCNNDeep LearningFederated Learning
